IEC 60076 — Acceptance tests

FAT and SAT: factory and site tests

VMS Energy documentally analyzes the factory acceptance tests (FAT) and performs the site tests (SAT) before the final energized.

IEC 60076-1 §11.1

Transformation ratio

Measurement of the transformation ratio at each tap. Tolerance: ±0.5% with respect to the nominal value.

IEC 60076-1 §11.2

Winding resistance

Measurement at known temperature in each winding. Allows you to calculate load losses and verify connection integrity.

IEC 60076-1 §11.4

Impedance and load losses

Short circuit test: determines Uk% and PCC. Tolerance: ±5% in losses, ±7.5% in Uk% for < 630 kVA.

IEC 60076-1 §11.5

No-load losses and no-load current

Test at nominal voltage and frequency. Determine P0 and I0%. Key to projecting energy costs in continuous operation.

IEC 60076-3

Dielectric test – applied voltage

Application of AC voltage for 60 s between windings and ground. Verifies integrity of the insulation system.

IEC 60076-5

Short circuit resistance

Dynamic short circuit test with asymmetric peak current. Verify that the transformer withstands electromagnetic forces during a fault.

Frequently asked questions

Most common technical doubts

What rules govern industrial transformers?
The series IEC 60076 is the main international standard: IEC 60076-1 (general requirements), 60076-2 (temperature), 60076-3 (dielectric insulation and testing), 60076-5 (short circuit resistance) and IEC 600 76-11 for dry transformers. In Mexico it is complemented by NOM-003-ENER (efficiency) and CFE L0000-06 for CFE projects.
What does the vector group Dyn11 mean?
D = primary winding in delta (Delta), and = devanado secundario en estrella (wye), N = accessible neutral on the secondary, 11 = 330° phase shift (equivalent to −30°) between phase voltages. The Dyn11 is the standard group for MV/LV distribution transformers because the accessible neutral allows for single-phase distribution and system grounding.
What is the difference between dry transformer and oil?
The dry transformer uses epoxy resin as insulation: suitable for indoors, fire-risk zones and high-level bathrooms; does not require containment. oil transformer uses dielectric mineral oil: higher cooling efficiency, higher power range (up to 100 MVA+) and lower cost per kVA, but requires {Quintto and more space. For > 4000 m we recommend dry with derating or consult the manufacturer.
What acceptance tests are performed according to IEC 60076-1?
The mandatory routine tests are: (1) transformation ratio, (2) winding resistance, (3) short-circuit impedance and load losses, (4) no-load losses and no-load current, (5) applied voltage dielectric test. Optional type tests: BIL impulse, temperature and noise test. VMS Energy accompanies the client in the factory FAT and executes the site SAT tests.
What is bil and how is it selected?
The BIL (Basic Impulse Insulation Level) is the maximum impulse voltage that supports the transformer expressed in kV crest. It is selected according to the nominal voltage of the system and the stormy area of activity: the higher the keraunic level (storm days per year), the greater the bil and the more important the coordination with ZnO lightning rod. For 34.5 kV the standard bil is 200 kV; for 115 kV is 550 kV.
