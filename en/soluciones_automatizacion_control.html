Challenges in the sector

What complicates the life of the control manager in a continuous plant...

01

Integration of heterogeneous protocols without downtime

Lines with Allen-Bradley PLC, Siemens S7 and Emerson DCS coexist without data exchange between control islands. Integration via gateway or migration implies a rigorous FAT/SAT that no generalist provider can guarantee without multi-brand experience.

02

Logic modifications in continuous critical process

Updating a PLC program or a DCS control block in an operating plant requires a validated rollback plan, a minimum intervention window and functional testing of each interlock before moving to production. An omission can result in an unscheduled stop or accident.

03

Galley turbines with Woodward controllers without internal support

Woodward 505, 2301D and MicroNet continue to operate in hundreds of Mexican plants without internal staff to master fine-tuning PID loops, load-speed rule and critical alarm diagnosis. Each instability event is an unexpected risk of shooting.

Specialized cable for automation and control

In automation the cable is the nervous network: every analog signal, every fieldbus, every control loop depends on the correct construction. One misspecified display or untwisted pair and the control network becomes unpredictable. We supply the four KEI / Helukabel families to cover the complete control network, integrated into the EPC scope.

Instrumentation

Analog Process Signals

Shielded cables for 4–20 mA signals, RTD/Pt100 thermometers, HART transmitters and process signals. Twisted pairs with individual and global shielding, low capacitance to preserve signal integrity over long loops.

Field buses

Industrial data: Profibus, Modbus, Ethernet

Specialized cables for Profibus PA/DP, Profinet, Modbus, EtherCAT and industrial Ethernet. Bus-specific constructions — controlled impedance, standard twisted pair, and protocol-compliant shielding.

EMC for VFD

EMC cable for drives

Shielded cable for power supply and signals from drives (VFD). Copper shield that channels common mode currents generated by PWM switching and prevents interference in the shared control network.

Electrical rooms

LSZH for control rooms and tunnels

Low Smoke Zero Halogen cables for control rooms, electrical rooms, PLC/SCADA racks and cable tunnels. Low smoke, no toxic halogen gases — protects equipment and personnel in confined spaces with high wiring density.

Energy and power management

EMS/PMS: Generation, Backup and Demand Control

When a plant combines grid, generators, UPS, BESS or solar, someone has to decide in real time which source powers which load. We specify, program and integrate energy and power management systems with platforms from DEIF, Woodward and Schneider Electric, depending on the scale and existing infrastructure.

Startup, synchronization and load distribution

Automatic start and stop of groups, synchronization between machines and with the network, active and reactive load distribution, N+1 redundancy.

Load shedding

Priority disconnection due to lack of capacity, low frequency or loss of a source, with staggered reconnection.

Demand management with BESS

Peak shaving, off-peak consumption shift and zero injection control into the grid.

Integration to SCADA/DCS

Communication through industrial protocols, alarms and event registration for analysis of each transfer or trip.

Applicable experience: load segregation system of the Akal-J process center (Diavaz), load sharing of five Wärtsilä motor generators at the P.S.S. Chemul and integrated automatic transfers to SCADA in Nohoch-A2. See how it fits into the Electrical Availability Architecture.
