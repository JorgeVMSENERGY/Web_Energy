Our commitment

Sustainability as a way of doing business

At VMS Energy we understand sustainability as a strategic element to generate long-term value and contribute to the development of our collaborators, clients, communities and environment. Our commitment is based on integrating environmental, social and governance (ESG) criteria into operations and decision-making, promoting a culture based on responsibility, ethics, continuous improvement and the efficient use of resources.

We take care of our environment

Environmental performance, energy efficiency, emissions management and efficient use of resources, within our Comprehensive Management System.

We develop our people

Safe and healthy work spaces, risk prevention and talent development of our collaborators.

We act with integrity

Business ethics, transparency, responsible supply chain and contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals.

We seek that sustainability is not only a commitment, but a way of doing business and generating value for the future.

Our own operation

What we do behind closed doors

Before talking about what we reduce in other people's facilities, this is what we measure and manage in our own. The 2025 data in this section comes from Sustainability Report 2025, which you can download on this same page.

Certified energy management

Energy efficiency management system ISO 50001 from 2023, with its own policy for monitoring and reducing consumption. In 2025 the facility's solar panels will generate 9,970 kWh. Initiatives of the year: maintenance of these panels, replacement with higher-saving lamps and automation of the turning on and off of climates. Electrical consumption is followed by period and compared against the previous year.

Green Path and Fleet Management

The teleworking modality compensates the footprint of the home-work journey, with monthly measurement of CO₂ and a minimum target of 30 %; In 2025 the result was 40 %. In the fleet, the distance fell from 60,933 to 46,188 km and consumption from 4,301 to 4,125 liters. The indicator per kilometer worsened, and that is why the next measures established are route optimization, preventive maintenance and, in 2026, the incorporation of our first flotilla of hybrid vehicles.

Waste hierarchy

Procedures for separation and disposal of hazardous and non-hazardous waste, with training for collaborators. Hazardous items — batteries, fluorescent lamps, electrical equipment — are delivered to an authorized service for incineration, confinement, co-processing or recycling. The hierarchy goes from reduce A discard, with awareness campaigns, leaf reuse mailbox and energy recovery through solar panels.

  1. 2021We incorporate solar panels to take advantage of renewable energy and move towards a more efficient operation.
  2. 2025We implemented trees in a median near our facilities and we maintain its care and maintenance.
  3. 2026We incorporated our first fleet of hybrid vehicles, towards more efficient mobility.

The generation and efficiency projects that we execute for our customers — industrial solar, non-solar green generation and energy efficiency — reduce emissions in each customer's installation, not ours. SOLUTIONS.

Where our mechanisms are more consolidated

  • Prohibition of child labor. Code of Ethics and Conduct and hiring controls in accordance with labor legislation.
  • Right to a healthy environment. ISO 14001, ISO 50001 and environmental impact matrix.
  • I work for contractors. ISO 45001 and security procedures also applied to third party personnel.

What we are working on

  • Indicators. Move from existing mechanisms to traceable indicators on equality and non-discrimination, decent work, privacy and data protection, and reparation.

  • Repair mechanisms. Measure the operation of the reporting channel: cases received, attended to, times and measures adopted, with confidentiality.

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most about sustainability

What environmental certifications does VMS Energy have?
VMS Energy has ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System, registration GSC14KMX277) and ISO 50001:2018 (Energy Management, registration GSC50KMX101), complemented by ISO 45001 (security), ISO 37001 (anti-bribery) and ISO 9001 (quality). The 5 certifications are audited annually by an accredited external body.
Where do I download the sustainability report?
The Sustainability Report 2025 is available free of charge in PDF format on this page. Click on the "Download full report" button in the previous section. The document includes environmental, social and governance metrics of the annual cycle.
How does VMS Energy manage its carbon footprint?
Through the Environmental Management System ISO 14001, we identify significant environmental aspects and impacts in each project. This includes measurement of direct and indirect emissions, waste reduction, transportation efficiency and energy consumption. Additionally, our solar energy and green generation projects contribute to the reduction of emissions at our clients' facilities.
What clean energy projects does VMS Energy carry out?
We execute three main lines: (1) Industrial Solar Energy — large-scale photovoltaic systems with network integration and remote monitoring. (2) Non-Solar Green Generation — wind, hydro micro-centrals and other renewable sources. (3) Energy Efficiency ISO 50001 — energy audits, VFDs, the UPS/Bscs systems and electrical distribution optimization to reduce consumption and emissions.
Does VMS Energy have a certified anti-snare policy?
Yes, we have ISO 37001:2025 (registration GSC37KMX131) that establishes an Anti-Bribery Management System. This is complemented by a mandatory Code of Ethics and Conduct, an Internal Anti-Laundering Policy and a suggestions/complaints box with a confidential service channel.
Can I request an energy efficiency audit?
Yes. Our engineering team conducts industrial energy audits under the framework ISO 50001. The service includes energy baseline survey, identification of improvement opportunities, ROI calculation and implementation proposal. Contact us through contacto@vmsenergy.com or call us at +52 33 3813 0739.
