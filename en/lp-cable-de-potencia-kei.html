Selection guide

XLPE or EPR? It is not a catalog question — it is a context question.

Both insulations operate at 90 °C in normal regime and 250 °C in short circuit. The difference is in the environment and the type of application.

Criterion LCA EPR
Dielectric strength Superior Good
Flexibility Minor Superior
Mobile equipment (trailing) ✗ Not recommended ✓ Ideal
Humid environments Good Better
Substations and fixed lines Ideal Supported
Relative cost Minor Major
VMS Recommendation Substations, main feeders, fixed medium voltage lines Mining (trailing cables), moving equipment, environments with severe humidity

Frequently asked questions

What information do I need to quote?

Operating voltage (U0/U in kV), total length per circuit, type of installation (band, duct, buried), ambient temperature of the site, required standard (IEC, NMX-J, CFE) and delivery schedule. With this data we contact you in less than 24 hours; the time of the quote depends on the scope.

XLPE or EPR for my project?

Depends on the application. XLPE is ideal for fixed installations, substations and main feeders. EPR is superior in mobile equipment (trailing cables in mining), high humidity environments and applications that require greater flexibility. Consult the Selection guide from this page or ask us for technical advice.

Do they handle domestic wire?

Yes. Condumex and Viakon (national manufacturing under NMX-J) are available for low voltage and control when the project requires national component, short delivery times or NOM-001-SEDE compliance. For medium and high voltage, KEI and Südkabel are the reference manufacturers.

Can you coordinate deliveries by construction phase?

Yes. Delivery by milestones is our standard in EPC projects. We coordinate supply schedule, coil traceability, technical documentation by batch and check of the personal data of the company before installation.

What documentation does the supply include?

Factory test certificate (individual and type tests), cable data sheet, length report per coil, traceability labeling and, if the project requires it, specific documentation for CFE, PEMEX, ISO audits or health audit in the food industry.

Does VMS also install or only supply?

Both options are available. As an EPC contractor, we integrate supply, installation, testing and commissioning under a single contract. If the project already has an installation contractor, we can act only as a technical supplier with the same rigor.
