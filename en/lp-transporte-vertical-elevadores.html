Vertical transportation

Maintenance and repair of multi-brand elevators for properties that cannot be stopped

Diagnosis, preventive and corrective maintenance, repair, replacement of components and commissioning of elevators of any brand. Direct and permanent attention from an electrical and electromechanical engineering team, with the same discipline with which we maintain substations and industrial plants.

Request diagnostic See case in hospitals
Multi-brand Hospitals and public buildings Diagnosis, repair and commissioning
The problem

A standing elevator is not a bother: in a hospital it is a transfer of patient that does not occur

The properties with the most vertical traffic usually also have the most heterogeneous equipment: elevators from different eras, manufacturers and support levels. These are the situations we see most frequently.

Several brands in one property

Each manufacturer with its own contract, its own response time and its own spare parts. A single multi-brand supplier simplifies management and accountability.

Teams without a current contract

Elevators whose contract with the manufacturer has ended and which operate “as long as they last”, without a maintenance program or a record of their actual condition.

Recurrent failures without root cause

Intermittent stoppages that are “resolved” by restarting control. Without electrical and mechanical diagnosis, the failure returns at the worst moment.

Obsolete components

Discontinued controllers, drives and door operators that lengthen each repair. The planned replacement of components prevents a spare part from stopping the equipment for weeks.

What we do

Integral vertical transport service

A single manager for the entire elevator service cycle, from first diagnosis to commissioning after repair.

Technical diagnosis

Electrical and mechanical review of all elevator systems, with status report, risks and recommended actions by priority.

Preventive maintenance

Periodic program of inspection, adjustment, cleaning, lubrication and testing of safety devices, adapted to the use and manufacturer's recommendations.

Corrective maintenance and repair

Failure attention with root cause diagnosis: control, traction, doors, leveling and power supply.

Replacement and installation of components

Replacement of damaged or obsolete components—control, drive, door operator, keypads, signaling and safety devices—with their integration into existing equipment.

Commissioning

Adjustments, functional and safety tests and return to service of the elevator after each repair or replacement, with a record of what was done.

Personalized and permanent attention

An assigned team who knows their equipment and real estate, with continuous monitoring and direct communication, not a call center.

Where we intervene

From the engine room to the pit, including power supply

An elevator is a complete electromechanical system. We review it as such, and as electrical contractors we also review what almost no one reviews: the acometide, the protections and the backrest that feed it.

Elevator feeding is part of our specialty: Power Quality, Emergency and transfer plants and Electrical maintenance.

Maintenance schedule

Maintenance that adjusts to the actual use of the equipment

A hospital elevator with hundreds of daily trips does not remain the same as one office. We define the frequency through transit, seniority and manufacturer’s recommendations.

FrequencyActivities
Periodic routineGeneral inspection, cleaning, lubrication, inspection of doors and operators, leveling, keypads, lighting and emergency communication
Scheduled reviewCable and pulley wear, brake adjustment, controller and drive inspection, electrical measurements and power board thermography
Security testingSpeed limiter and parachutes, shock absorbers, limit switches, door interlocks, with results report
Corrective and replacementFailure diagnosis, repair, replacement and installation of components and commissioning with functional tests

Reference regulations: NOM-001-SEDE in force for the electrical installation of the elevator and its backup circuits, NOM-053-SCFI-2000 for safety specifications of electric traction elevators, and the recommendations of each manufacturer.

Frequently asked questions

Do they take lifts of any brand?
Yes. We work in a multi-brand way: we have served OTIS, KONE (Sabiem) and Alamex equipment, among others, even in the same property. We are not distributors or representatives of any manufacturer, so the technical recommendation does not depend on selling a brand.
Can you give service to an elevator that you didn't install or whose contract with the manufacturer ended?
Yes. That is the most common case. We start with a technical diagnosis of the equipment to know its actual condition, and from there we propose the maintenance program and the necessary repairs or replacements.
What does the diagnosis include?
Inspection of the traction machine and the brake, the controller and the variator, the cables and pulleys, the cabin and the door operator, the landing doors and their interlocks, the pit and the electrical supply. We deliver a report with the status of each system, the risks detected and the actions recommended by priority.
Do you give service outside Jalisco and Campeche?
Yes. We serve projects throughout Mexico; for example, we serve hospitals and health services offices in the State of Durango.
Should a hospital elevator operate on the emergency plant?
In hospitals, at least the elevators intended for the transfer of patients are usually part of the loads that must be supported. As an electrical contractor we review the elevator along with its power, its protections and its transfer to the emergency plant, in accordance with the current NOM-001-SEDE.

Do you know what condition your elevators are really in?

We carry out a technical diagnosis of your equipment, of any brand, and we provide you with the status of each system with the recommended actions by priority.
