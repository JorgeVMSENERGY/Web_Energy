Our services

Electrical predictive maintenance services

We use non-invasive diagnostic technology to assess the actual condition of your electrical assets without interrupting production.

NETA MTS / ISO 18434

Infrared termography (IR)

Inspection with thermal imaging camera on electrical panels, substations, motors, transformers and MCC under load. Detects loose connections, damaged fuses, phase imbalance and conductor overload. Report with IR/visible images and priority classification according to delta-T.

IEC 60060-3/IEEE 400.2

VLF test on MV cables

Very low frequency test (0.1 Hz) on 5–35 kV XLPE/EPR cables. Detects water trees, voids and insulation degradation without damage to healthy cable. Includes leakage current monitoring (tan-delta) for quantitative evaluation of insulation status.

IEEE C57.12.91/NETA MTS

Transformer diagnosis

Complete battery of tests: TTR (transformation ratio), winding resistance, insulation resistance and polarization index (PI), power factor of the dielectric and dielectric strength of the oil. Green/yellow/red traffic light evaluation and intervention recommendation.

IEC 61000-4 / IEEE 519

Power quality and harmonics

Measurement of THDi, THDv, power factor, flicker, voltage dips and transients with a power quality analyzer. Identifies sources of distortion, affected equipment and proposes solutions (passive filters, APF, capacitor bank).

IEC 60599/ASTM D3612

DGA analysis of oil (transformers)

Analysis of dissolved gases (H₂, CH₄, C₂H₂, CO, CO₂) in transformer oil to detect partial discharges, internal arcs and overheating of insulating paper. Interpretation according to the Duval and Rogers method for precise diagnosis of the type of internal failure.

NET MTS/IEC 62271

Tests of MV switches and switchgear

Measurement of switch opening and closing time, static contact resistance (microohmmeter), trip coil test, spring mechanism verification and insulation test. Compatible with SF₆, vacuum and air switches.

Methodology

Predictive diagnosis process

1. Asset collection

Inventory of equipment to be diagnosed: transformers, panels, MV cables and switches. Review of maintenance history, previous failures and operating conditions (load, temperature, age).

2. Inspection and testing

Execution of thermography under load, VLF tests without interrupting operation (MV cables), and battery of acceptance tests on transformers and switches during scheduled shutdown window.

3. Analysis and diagnosis

Interpretation of results according to NETA MTS, IEEE and manufacturer. Classification of each asset in green/yellow/red traffic light with technical reason and comparison against commissioning reference values.

4. Report and action plan

Delivery of technical report with IR images, measurement graphs, condition table and prioritized action plan. The report can be the basis for the corrective maintenance budget for the following period.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about electrical predictive maintenance

When is it recommended to test a VLF on medium voltage cables?

VLF (Very Low Frequency, 0.1 Hz) testing is recommended every 3–5 years on MV cables in service, after any cable failure or repair, and as an acceptance test in new installations. It is especially critical in XLPE and EPR cables that accumulate waterlogging over time.

What does infrared trimography detect in electrical installations?

IR thermography detects hot spots due to loose connections, deteriorated fuses, load imbalance, overloading of conductors, incipient failures in transformers and motors, and defective insulation in switchboards and substations. A hot spot of more than 30°C above the environment is a sign of urgent corrective action.

What does the full diagnosis of a power transformer include?

Complete diagnosis includes: TTR (turns ratio), cold winding resistance, insulation resistance (megger at 1 kV or 5 kV) and polarization index (PI), dielectric power factor, oil DGA analysis and dielectric strength test. A report is delivered with a condition light and intervention recommendation.

Can you run VLF tests on offshore platforms?

Yes. We have conducted VLF tests on MV 4 160 V cable systems on Pemex/Cotemar platforms in the Gulf of Mexico, under offshore safety protocols and coordinating stop windows with production equipment.

How often should electrical predictive maintenance be performed?

The NETA MTS recommends annual electrical thermography for critical installations and transformer inspections every 1–3 years. In aggressive environments or installations with high criticality, a semiannual frequency is recommended. MV cables under load must undergo VLF every 3 years.

What is DGA analysis and when is it required?

The DGA analyzes the gases dissolved in the transformer oil (H₂, CH₄, C₂H₂, CO, etc.) to detect partial electric discharges, internal arcs and overheating of insulating paper. It is mandatory in transformers greater than 1 MVA and is recommended annually in critical equipment.

What is the difference between isolation resistance (megger) and polarization index (PI)?

Insulation resistance measures the point condition of the insulation at 1 min. The PI is the ratio RI at 10 min / RI at 1 min. A PI ≥ 2.0 indicates insulation in good condition; between 1.0–2.0 is questionable; less than 1.0 indicates humidity or severe contamination. The PI eliminates the influence of temperature on the reading.
