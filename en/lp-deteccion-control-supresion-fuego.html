EPC fire solutions

You need a partner capable of integrating fire protection with the operational reality of the site: panels, substations, electrical rooms, control rooms, skids, industrial processes and classified areas.

Engineering and selection

Definition of architecture, detection points, alarm logic and suppression philosophy according to the criticality of the asset.

Integration and installation

Assembly, wiring, interconnection of panels, field devices, loops, release of agents and communication with existing systems.

Testing and commissioning

Functional protocols, cause-effect sequences, alarm verification, interlocks and document delivery for operation and maintenance.

Specialties

Electric

Frequently asked questions

We resolve the most common doubts before the technical diagnosis.

What does the fire detection, control and suppression solution include?

We can cover engineering, equipment selection, supply, integration, installation, functional testing, commissioning and document delivery. The exact scope depends on the asset, risk and existing infrastructure on site.

Do they attend projects for electrical rooms, boards, MCC or substations?

Yes. The Hierarchy is designed precisely for business conversations related to critical electrical infrastructure, where fire protection must coexist with operational continuity, alarms and control systems.

Can they develop ranges for Dochac environments or classified areas?

Yes. We evaluate site conditions, type of risk and compatibility of the solution with the operating environment to structure a proposal more appropriate than a generic offer.

Can the proposal be integrated with already installed systems?

Yes. One of the commercial differentials is precisely to integrate fire protection with existing panels, electrical infrastructure, automation, monitoring and control logics where it applies.

What do you need to prepare a quote or initial diagnosis?

It helps to have site location, type of asset, criticality of the operation, environmental conditions, basic information on the current system, and project objective. With that we can better guide the first commercial scope.

Does your plant have updated fire suppression systems?

Design, supply and installation of fire detection and suppression systems for industry and electrical rooms.
