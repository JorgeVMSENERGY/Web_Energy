Why work with us

Beyond the position: a culture of execution, safety and professional growth supported by our QHSE+ Comprehensive Management System (ISO 9001, 14001, 37001, 45001 and 50001).

Impact projects

We work in oil & gas, mining, energy infrastructure and manufacturing. Each project is a real technical opportunity, not a desk.

Safety first

We comply with ISO 45001 and strict HSE procedures. The safety of our team is non-negotiable, in the office and in the field.

Professional growth

Continuing training plan, sponsored technical certifications, and clear career paths from operations to leadership.

Multidisciplinary team

Engineering, operations, automation, maintenance, HSE and administration working together. You learn from those who are next to you.
