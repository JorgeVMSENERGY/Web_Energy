Challenges in the sector

What keeps the electrical maintenance manager awake at a food plant

Electrical engineering in food and beverages faces three simultaneous pressures that do not exist in other sectors: continuity without margin of error, auditable safety and energy efficiency with a direct impact on margin.

01

Line continuity — no margin

An unplanned two-hour power cut on a filling line, edging or cold chamber can mean loss of complete batch, rejection by quality control and customer penalty. In continuous process, the cost of an unprogrammed stoppage far exceeds the cost of any preventive maintenance program. There is no room to improvise with plant electricity.

02

Safety in electrical installations

The COFEPRIS, BRC Global Standard and FSSC 22000 audits include the state of electrical installations in their evaluations. Panels without adequate IP grade in humid areas, wiring without sealed conduit in process areas or equipment without NOM registration can lead to major observations or temporary closure. The electrical installation must be as auditable as the production process itself.

03

Energy cost over operating margin

With HM/HS rates on the rise, oversized motors running at part load and power factor below 0.90, additional charges on the CFE bill are real and avoidable. Harmonics greater than 5% THD reduce the useful life of transformers and CCMs. Electrical efficiency requires specific engineering, not just intention.

Specialized services

Electrical solutions for each stage of the production process

From the main medium voltage power supply to the control of each motor in the filling line — we integrate the entire electrical chain under the same technical manager.

MV/HV substations for process plant

Design and installation of substations at 13.8 kV, 34.5 kV and 115 kV with dry or oil transformers, shielded switchgear and selective protections. We comply with NOM-001-SEDE and coordination of protections for critical process loads.

, NOM-001-SEDE · IEC 62271 · I

Speed changers for process

Selection, installation and commissioning of VFDs for product pumps, compressors, conveyors and mixers. IP54/IP65 cabinets compatible with CIP/SIP cleaning environments. Integration with SCADA and Profibus, Modbus, EtherNet/IP protocols.

, IEC 61800 · NEMA 4X · Siemens · ABB · TRIOL

Automated MCC and process control

Motor control centers with soft starters, integrated drives and PLC for automation of production lines. Design under IEC 61439 with segregated compartments, communication bus and real-time monitoring.

, IEC 61439 · IEC 60947 · Profibus · Modbus R

Electrical predictive maintenance

Preventive-predictive maintenance programs with infrared thermography (NETA MTS), VLF tests on MV cables, DGA analysis on transformers and power quality measurement (harmonics, flicker, unbalance). Reports with risk traffic light and action plan.

, NETA MTS · IEC 60599 · IEEE 519 · ISO 18434

Power factor correction

Design and installation of automatic capacitor banks to maintain PF >0.95 and eliminate reactive energy charges in the CFE rate. Previous analysis of harmonics with passive filters when required. Typical ROI: 12–24 months.

, IEC 60831 · IEEE 18 · NOM-016-ENER · HM

Industrial lighting and energy efficiency

Industrial LED lighting projects in production areas, warehouses and cold rooms with compliance with NOM-025-STPS (illuminance levels per area). Energy efficiency studies and audits under NOM-016-ENER to reduce total electrical consumption.

, NOM-025-STPS · NOM-016-ENER · I
