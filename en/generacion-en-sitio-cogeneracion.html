Regulatory and fiscal framework

What you should know before generating your own energy

Permits (Electric Sector Law, 2025)

  • Plants less than 0.7 MW: distributed generation with interconnection contract, without permission from the CNE.
  • Self-consumption of 0.7 MW or more: requires generation permit from the National Energy Commission.
  • Between 0.7 and 20 MW in interconnected self-consumption, a simplified procedure applies.
  • Any interconnection must comply with the Network Code in protections, energy quality and control.

Tax incentive

The Income Tax Law (ISR, Article 34, Section XIII) allows a 100% deduction for machinery and equipment used to generate power from renewable sources, including biomass such as bagasse, or through efficient power cogeneration systems, provided the equipment operates for at least five years.

The qualification as efficient cogeneration and the application of the deduction must be validated with the regulator and with your tax advisor.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run turnkey cogeneration projects?
Our proven experience is in the electrical and control part of generation: synchronization, excitation, governors, protections, interconnection and start-up. In new cogeneration projects we work together with the engine or turbine supplier and the thermal specialist, and we assume the electrical and control scope under a single contract.
Can you modernize the control of a Turbogenerator without changing the turbine?
Yes. It is one of our most frequent works in ingenuity: replacing obsolete mechanical or electronic governors with digital controllers, retaining actuators when technically feasible, and adding synchronization and communication with the plant control system.
What is needed to export surplus to the network?
A synchronization and interconnection protection scheme that complies with the Network Code, the corresponding contract or permit according to capacity (the threshold of 0.7 MW defines whether permission is required from the CNE) and the corresponding measurement. The economic viability of the export depends on the current rules of consideration.
Do they serve plants outside Mexico?
Yes. We have performed synchronization, excitement and control work in ingenuity and generation plants in Guatemala, El Salvador and Honduras.

Is your own generation ready for the next harvest or the next strike?

We review control, synchronization and protections of your generation plant and provide you with a prioritized intervention plan.
