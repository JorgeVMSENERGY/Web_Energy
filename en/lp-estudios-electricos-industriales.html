Types of study

Electrical studies we carry out

Each study generates a technical report with the ETAP/SKM model, results, coordination curves and adjustment recommendations.

IEEE 399/IEC 61363

Power flow (Load Flow)

Calculates bus voltages, loadability of conductors and transformers, losses and power factor in steady state. Identify bottlenecks before expansions. Required as a basis for all other studies.

IEC 60909/ANSI C37.13

Short Circuit

Determines three-phase, two-phase and single-phase fault currents in each node. Verifies the interrupting capacity of switches, fuses and relays against the maximum possible short circuit current. Prerequisite for equipment selection.

IEEE 242/IEC 60255

Coordination of protections

Adjustment of time-current curves of relays, thermomagnetic switches and fuses to guarantee selectivity: the device closest to the fault operates first, minimizing the de-energized area. Deliver coordination curves in TCC format.

NFPA 70E/IEEE 1584-2018

Arc Flash

Calculates the incident energy (cal/cm²) and arc protection limit (APB) per panel and section. Determines the required PPE level and generates NFPA 70E labels for each work point. Required for electrical safety programs.

IEC 61000-3-12 / IEEE 519

Power quality and harmonics

Analysis of THDi, THDv, power factor, voltage dips, flicker and harmonic resonance. Identify sources of distortion and propose solutions: passive, active or APF filters. Compliant with IEEE 519-2022 requirements.

Lloyd's / DNV-GL

Studies for platforms and ships

Electrical studies under classification house rules (Lloyd's Register, DNV-GL, ABS) for offshore platforms, vessels and floating units. History with Pemex/Cotemar in the Gulf of Mexico.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about electrical studies

What rules govern the study of electric arc?
NFPA 70E (Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace) and IEEE 1584-2018 (Guide for Performing Arc-Flash Hazard Calculations) are the reference standards. The study determines the incident energy (cal/cm²), arc protection limit (APB), and personal protective equipment (PPE) required for each panel and system section.
How often should the coordination study be updated?
NFPA 70E recommends reviewing the study every 5 years or when significant system changes are made: new loads, change of transformers, addition of distributed generation, modification of the CFE supply network or replacement of switches. outdated studies can result in incorrect protection configurations and risk of electric arc.
What software do you use for electrical studies?
VMS Energy uses ETAP and SKM PowerTools for the modeling of industrial electrical systems, including power flow, short circuit, protection coordination and electric arc analysis according to IEEE 1584-2018. The model is delivered to the customer in editable format for future updates.
How long does it take to conduct an electrical study?
The time depends on the size of the system and the availability of information (single-line, relay data, transformer specifications). A complete coordination + short circuit + arc flash study for a medium plant (50–200 nodes) takes between 3 and 6 weeks from data collection to the delivery of the final report.
