Scope of service

Services for electrical substations

We cover the complete life cycle: from conceptual engineering to periodic major maintenance.

EPC Construction

Conceptual and detailed design, supply of transformers, switches, switchgear and bars, civil works, electromechanical assembly, FAT/SAT tests and turnkey commissioning.

Rehabilitation and expansion

Replacement of switches, disconnectors and transformers. Expansion of operational capacity. Modernization of protection and control systems (SEL, Schweitzer, ABB REF numerical relays).

Major maintenance

Preventive and corrective maintenance: primary electrical tests, VLF, thermographic inspection, cleaning and adjustment of switches, calibration of protection relays and coordination of protections.

Electrical acceptance tests

TTR (transformation relation), devanate resistance, dielectric rigidity, vacuum and load losses, VLF to MV and HV cables, short circuit impedance test.

Synchronization switchgear and interlocks

Design and installation of synchronization switchgear for cogeneration systems and generation plants. Safety interlocks, mechanical and electrical interlocks for shared substations.

ALS/SF₆ Switches

Supply, installation and rehabilitation of high voltage switches SF6 (ALS 5315, ALS 56-235) for CFE Transmission and industrial substations. Reload and SF6 tests.

Methodology

EPC execution process

Engineering and design

Field survey, short circuit study, coordination of protections, single-line plans, calculation reports and equipment specifications.

Equipment supply

Transformers, SF6 or vacuum switches, MV switchgear, bars, control boards and protection. Follow up on the factory at the factory.

Construction and installation

Civil works (foundations, cubes), electromechanical assembly, control and instrumentation wiring, and ground connections.

Testing and commissioning

SAT tests according to NETA MTS: TTR, resistance, VLF, dielectric strength, relay calibration, functional test and transfer of operation to the client.

Electrical tests

Acceptance and diagnostic tests that we perform

IEEE C57.12/IEC 60076

TTR — Transformation Reason

Check the nominal rotation ratio and the polarity of the devanados in all the OCTC/OLTC taps.

NET MTS/IEC 60076

Winding resistance

Detects loose joints, broken conductor or thermal deterioration in coils. Measurement with Kelvin bridge or micro-ohmmeter.

IEEE 400.2/IEC 60502

VLF — OW Low Frequency

Dielectric test at 0.1 Hz in MV cables. Detects deterioration of XLPE insulation without risk of destruction as in DC.

IEC 60076-1 §11.5

Dielectric strength (hypot)

voltage applied for 1 minute to verify integrity of the main and interphase insulation.

ANSI/NET MTS

Contact resistance

Measurement of resistance in the main circuit of switches and disconnectors to detect burned or loose contacts.

IEC 62271/ANSI C37

Opening/closing time

Verification of SF6 and vacuum circuit breaker operation times with trigger circuit analyzer.

Frequently asked questions

Common doubts about electrical substations

What does the major maintenance of a substation include?
Major maintenance covers: primary electrical tests (TTR, winding resistance, dielectric strength), VLF tests of primary and secondary circuits, inspection and diagnosis of switches and disconnectors, adjustment and verification of protection relays, cleaning and painting of the structure, and issuance of a condition report with recommendations.
What are VLF tests in substations?
VLF ( Mania Low Frequency) is a dielectric test technique that applies alternating voltage at very low frequency (0.1 Hz) to MV and devanated cables. Detecta deterioration of the insulation LEDL without the risk of destruction that comes with traditional CD voltage. VMS Energy routinely applies it to primary and secondary circuits of industrial substations and offshore platforms.
Does VMS Energy build substations from scratch?
Yeah. VMS Energy carries out complete EPC projects for electrical substations: conceptual and detail engineering, equipment supply (transformers, SF6 switches, switchgear, bars), civil works, electromechanical assembly, NETA acceptance tests and commissioning. References include the construction of substations for PIASA in Veracruz and the major maintenance of substation of 69 kV in Jalisco.
What levels of stress do you work with?
VMS Energy works with substations from medium voltage (4.16–34.5 kV) to high voltage (115 kV). We have executed projects in 4 160 V, 6.6 kV, 13.8 kV, 23 kV, 34.5 kV and 69 kV for industry, oil & gas and ingenuity, as well as rehabilitation works for ALS switches for CFE Transmission.
