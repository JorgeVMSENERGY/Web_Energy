Supply Portfolio

Topologies of variable frequency drives that we supply and install

Selection depends on voltage level, power, EMC requirements, regenerative capacity and process communication protocol.

LV standard

VFD 2 levels — VSI (Voltage Source Inverter)

More widespread topology. Rect. diodes + DC bus + IGBT bridge. Range 0.37–2 500 kW, voltages 220–690 V AC. THDi without filter 35–45%; with 20–25% line reactor; with LCL filter <5%. Suitable for most pumps, fans, compressors and belts.

220–690VUp to 2 500 kWIGBTLine reactorOptional LCL filter
Medium Voltage

VFD 3 levels — NPC (Neutral Point Clamped)

For medium voltage motors 2.3–6.6 kV and powers from 315 kW to several MW. The multilevel design reduces dv/dt over the motor winding and improves the waveform (THDv <5% without extra filter). Requires input isolation transformer.

2.3–6.6 kV315 kW–several MWIGBT/IGCTTHDv <5%Xfmr input
Regenerative

AFE — Active Front End (active rectification)

Replaces the diode bridge with an active IGBT bridge. Key advantages: THDi <3% (IEEE 519), unity power factor (0.99) and energy recovery to the grid in regenerative mode. Ideal for cranes, elevators, presses, drilling machines and applications with frequent or continuous braking.

THDi <3%FP = 0.99RegenerativeIEEE 5194 quadrants
Advanced settings

Multi-drive / Common DC Bus

Several inverters share a common DC bus powered by a centralized rectifier or shared AFE. The braking energy of one axle is reused on another motor axis. It is borderlined the number of rectifiers, improves overall THD and is optimal in machines with multiple coordinated axes (production lines, winders, looms).

Derating by altitude and temperature

Mexico has altitudes that significantly impact the current capacity of a drive. Applying the correct factors from the design avoids overtemperature and premature breakdowns.

Derating by altitude

Above 1 000 m a.s.l., apply −1% of rated current for every additional 100 m.

Protection functions

Integrated Protections (ANSI Designation)

Modern drives incorporate electronic protections that complement or replace external protection relays, reducing costs and response times.

ANSI 49

Overtemperature (thermal)

I²t thermal model of the motor and NTC/PTC sensor in the IGBT. Trip with adjustable pre-alarm.

ANSI 51

Outflow overcurrent

Instantaneous and delayed electronic limitation to protect motor windings and IGBTs.

ANSI 46

Phase imbalance

Detects output current asymmetry greater than the configured threshold; protects against winding failure.

ANSI 27

Network underlay

Trip with hysteresis when Vred falls below the threshold. Automatic reset configurable after replacement.

ANSI 59

Overvoltage of DC network / bus

DC bus monitoring; Activates braking resistor or regenerative mode before overvoltage trip.

ANSI 48

Incomplete start / rotor lock

Detects blocked motor or excessive load during starting; maximum force ramp with time limit.

Industrial integration

Supported communication protocols

Integration with PLC, DCS and SCADA is critical for process automation. We select drives with the native protocol of the existing control system.

Modbus RTU/TCP

Universal RS-485 or Ethernet standard. Compatible with all PLCs on the market. Up to 247 RTU nodes.

Profibus DP

Siemens protocol with wide penetration in the process industry. Up to 12 Mbps, cycle det. <1ms.

PROFINET

Siemens Industrial Ethernet. Real time IRT (<1 ms), ideal for motion axes and CNC machines.

EtherNet/IP

Allen-Bradley/Rockwell protocol. TCP/IP + CIP. Compatible with FactoryTalk and Studio 5000.

DeviceNet

CAN network for compact Rockwell PLCs. Trunk/branch topology, up to 64 nodes.

BACnet/LON

For HVAC systems and building management. Integration with BMS and energy monitoring systems.

CANopen/DriveProfile

Motion applications and process machinery. Axis synchronization with DSP402 profile.

OPC-UA/IIoT

Direct connectivity to IIoT platforms (Azure IoT Hub, AWS IoT, MindSphere) for process analytics.

Regulatory framework

Reference regulatory framework

The standards presented are of a information The rules applicable to each project — including national standards, requirements of the client or competent authority — are determined and agreed at the basic engineering stage in accordance with the specific requirements of the scope contracted.

IEC 61800-3

EMC for drives. Defines C1–C4 categories, conducted/radiated emissions limits and immunity requirements. Mandatory for CE Marking.

IEC 61800-5

Electrical and thermal safety. Insulation, protections, surface temperature and protective earth requirements.

IEC 61800-7

Communication profile. Generic interface for industrial networks (PROFIdrive, CiA402). Facilitates multi-brand integration with PLCs.

IEC 61000-3-12

Harmonics in MV/LV network >16 A. Establishes limits of THDi and individual harmonic currents at the point of common connection (PCC).

IEEE 519-2022

ANSI/IEEE harmonics standard. Reference in projects for North America. Sets THDv ≤5% and THDi according to short circuit ratio in the PCC.

UL 508C

Power Conversion Equipment. Required when the project follows NEC (NFPA 70) or the customer requires UL listing for equipment installed in the US or with NFPA code.

Frequently asked questions

Common doubts about frequency converters

How much energy savings does a frequency changer generate?
Thanks to the law of affinity (P ∝ n³), reducing the speed of a motor to 80% reduces consumption to 51%. For pumps and fans operating partially loaded, typical savings are 20 to 50% Regarding valve control or Munner. With a power price of 2.50–3.00 €2.50 – €30.00 €20.00 €50–500 kW engines, the return on investment is usually less than 2 years.
What rules govern the electromagnetic compatibility of VFDs?
IEC 61800-3 defines EMC requirements for variable speed drives. It sets categories C1–C4 according to the environment (residential vs. industrial) and the emission limits driven and radiated. For current harmonics, it applies IEC 61000-3-12 (equipment >16 A) e IEEE 519-2022 when the project follows NFPA/Aussi codes.
What is an AFE-type VFD and when should it be used?
A Active Front End (AFE) uses an active rectifier bridge (IGBT) instead of diodes, achieving THDi <3% It is suitable for applications with frequent braking (grills, elevators, presses) or where the quality of energy is critical. It has a cost 30–50% higher than a standard VFD, but it is amortized with the energy savings in braking and the elimination of dissipation resistances.
How does altitude affect the performance of a variator?
Above 1,000 m a.s.l., less dense air reduces the cooling of the drive. The standard rule is to apply a derating of −1% of the nominal current per 100 m additional. In Mexico City (2 240 m) the derating is ~12–15%. Some manufacturers offer special models up to 4,000 m without derating through reinforced ventilation design or overcalibrated cabinets. VMS Energy calculates the combined altitude + temperature factor for each project.

Yes.
