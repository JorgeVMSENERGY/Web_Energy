Specialised cable supply for mining

Each mine face requires a different cable construction. It is not the same specification for a cable shovel as it is for a substation feeder. These are the three KEI cable families with which we cover the complete mining operation, integrated into the EPC scope of VMS Energy.

Mobile equipment — Trailing EPR cables

Class 5 flexible conductor with EPR insulation for applications that continuously wind and unwind cable. Typical voltages 1.1–6.6 kV. Applications: electric shovels, mobile drills, hauling equipment, mobile conveyors. Resistant to abrasion, impacts and thermal cycles.

What is a cable flaying? →

Fixed Installation — XLPE Power Cable

XLPE insulation for fixed installations: internal substations, main feeders, crushing lines and mills. Voltages 6.6–22 kV (up to 33 kV in large projects). Constructions with steel reinforcement for buried sections or pipelines with mechanical risk.

Lighting, control and instrumentation

LSZH (low smoke, zero halogen) cables for tunnels and confined spaces, multiconductor control with EMC display for PLC/SCADA, and shielded instrumentation for analog signals. NOM-001-SEDE compliance and international standards.

Transformers for mining plant

Mine electrical distribution requires transformers that withstand dust, humidity, vibration, and—in high-altitude operations—adequate thermal derating. VMS Energy supplies, installs, tests and commissions power and distribution transformers with full EPC scope, including FAT/SAT testing and documented delivery under IEC 60076.

Substations for distribution in mine

Transformers < 2,500 kVA for powering MCC boards, bilge pumps, fans and process loads. Primary 6–34.5 kV → secondary 220–480 V. Standard Dyn11 Group. NER2 efficiency class (NOM-003-ENER).

Dry transformer — Socavón and risk areas

Class F or H epoxy resin for underground mines and fire risk areas. Maintenance-free dielectric liquid, IP20–IP54. Suitable for altitudes > 3,000 m with derating according to IEC 60076-11. Without containment bucket.

Power transformers for plant

Units > 2,500 kVA for SAG mills, concentrates and profit plants. Refrigeration ONAN, ONAF or OFAF according to available thermal load. Up to 100 MVA. Coordination of protections and short circuit studies included.
