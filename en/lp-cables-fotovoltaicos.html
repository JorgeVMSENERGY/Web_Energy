Section calculator for solar cable

In a solar park the section is almost never decided by the ampacity: it is decided by the Voltage drop. Here both are checked with the factory data of the KEI PV1-F, and it is said which of the two rules in your section.

As a guide: standard strings usually range in 4–6 mm², long or high current strings in 10–16 mm², and DC trunks in 25–50 mm². The figure below is You stage.

The calculation uses twice that length: in DC the current goes back and forth through two conductors.

The fall limit is set by park engineering, not the cable standard. 2% on the DC side is a common starting value.

KEI ampacity assumes conductor at 90°C and ambient at 40°C in air. In a closed tray, on the deck or with several circuits together, it must be lowered.

The catalog resistance is at 20 °C. At 90 °C copper resists a 28 % The calculation goes on the safe side.

Flexible tinned copper DC solar cable, with halogen-free cross-linked polyolefin insulation and sheath. KEI factory catalog data.

Standards and certification

Until 30 years UV resistance according to HD 605/A1 or DIN 53367, ozone according to EN 50396 8.1.3, and oils and alkalis according to EN 60811-2-1.

In work

Minimum curvature radius 4 times the diameter and maximum shooting force of 50 N/mm2. Halogen-free: low smoke and low toxicity if there is fire.

Sections

From 2.5 to 16 mm² are the catalog ones; from 1.5 to 240 mm² are tabulated and manufactured under project request. That's where the DC trunks come in.

Engineering Notes

Key criteria for PV selection

All measurements are reference for preliminary selection. Final choice depends on length of stretch, design current, installation method and project voltage drop limits.

Allowable current

Check base ampacity at 20 C and adjust by room temperature, grouping and watering.

Voltage drop

Validate the percentage allowed in DC and AC per tranche, considering total length and caliber.

Protection & security

Coordinate fuses, sectioning and grounding according to topology and inverters.

Temperature and derating

In rooftop or open field installations, the actual cable temperature may exceed 60°C. Apply ampacity correction factors for actual ambient site temperature to avoid premature insulation degradation.

Standard and certification

The PV1-F KEI cable complies with BS EN 50618, 2Pfg 1169/08.2007 and IEC 60228 Class 5. For projects with CFE (interconnection), verify that the park specification includes the standard required by the interconnection opinion.

Frequently asked questions — Photovoltaic cables

What cable section should I use in my solar park?

Depends on the length of the string, the short circuit current of the module (Isc) and the voltage drop limits of the project. For standard strings: 4–6 mm². For long strings or high current: 10–16 mm². For DC trunks: 25–50 mm². VMS does the sizing if you share the electrical design.

Does the PV1-F cable serve as a buried installation?

The standard PV1-F is not designed for direct buried installation. For underground sections in a solar park, cable with additional sheath (double insulation or protective pipe) is required. VMS can specify the correct solution for each leg.

How long are these cables?

The PV1-F KEI cable has an estimated useful life of 30 years under standard operating conditions, with resistance to UV rays, ozone and thermal cycling. It is compatible with the typical useful life of an industrial solar park.

Can you supply the cable together with the EPC solar?

Yes. VMS Energy runs turnkey EPC projects. The supply of photovoltaic cable is part of the EPC range: we specify it, we try and install it. You don't need to coordinate with a separate provider.

Do you handle AC (AC) cable for the investor's exit?

Yes. In addition to the photovoltaic DC cable, we supply MV power cable for the interconnection of the inverter/lift transformer with the interconnection substation. All the cable infrastructure of the park can be managed with VMS.

What standard does CFE require for interconnection of solar parks?

CFE reviews the electrical design through the interconnection opinion (CENACE/CFE). Cables on the AC side must meet LAPEM specifications for interconnection voltage level. VMS accompanies the process from basic engineering to approval of the opinion.
