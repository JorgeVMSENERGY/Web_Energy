The real industrial context

Your plant already generates data. The problem is that no one sees them.

Modern equipment (VFDs, CCMs, protective relays, energy meters) have communication ports that most plants have not connected. The result: operators who work with data from 4 hours ago, failures that are due to trends that no one saw, and energy consumption that no one optimizes.

Surprising failures

The motor failed but no one saw that the current had been rising for 3 weeks. With continuous monitoring, the alarm arrives before the fault.

Equipment without common protocol

Siemens PLC, ABB VFD and Schneider meter on the same line, but not talking to each other. Protocol gateways solve this without replacing any equipment.

Energy that is not measured

What is not measured, cannot be improved. Most energy efficiency projects start with a week of measuring to find out where the waste is.

IIoT solutions

From field data to the management dashboard

Industrial Protocol Gateways

Integration of legacy equipment through protocol conversion: Modbus RTU/TCP, Profibus DP, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, DNP3, BACnet, OPC-UA and MQTT. Brands: ANYBUS, RED LION, Moxa. Field installation and integration with existing SCADA or cloud platforms.

Industrial SCADA — design and programming

SCADA/HMI architecture design, screen programming, tag configuration, alarms, variable history and automatic reports. Platforms: SIEMENS WinCC, Wonderware, FactoryTalk. Integration with ERP via OPC-UA or REST API.

Continuous monitoring of machinery

Installation of vibration, temperature, current and speed sensors in motors, pumps, compressors and turbines. Integration of Bently Nevada 3500 System and BV7 portable analyzers with real-time monitoring platform. Alerts configurable by ISO 10816 thresholds.

Electrical energy monitoring

Installation of power quality meters (THD, FP, imbalance, flicker) with communication to the SCADA. Dashboard of consumption KPIs by line, machine or process. Identification of demand peaks, hours of highest consumption and low power factor equipment.

Automation of industrial processes

Design and implementation of control logic, automatic operation routines and start/stop sequences. Integration of PLC with vision systems, scales, drives and production management systems (MES/ERP).

Energy efficiency and carbon footprint

Consumption analysis, identification of savings opportunities, implementation of variable frequency drives in pumps and fans without variable load, and maximum demand configuration. Carbon footprint reporting aligned with GHG Protocol for your ESG strategy.

Technical reference

Typical architecture of an industrial IIoT project

Layer 1 — Field

Sensors, VFDs, PLCs, meters, protection relays with communication port

Layer 2 — Connectivity

Protocol gateways (ANYBUS, Moxa), industrial switches, industrial Ethernet network

Layer 3 — Control/SCADA

SCADA server (WinCC/FactoryTalk), OPC UA, process historian and alarm logic

Layer 4 — Management

Dashboard KPIs, energy reports, mobile alerts, integration with ERP / SAP

VMS Energy can integrate any of the 4 layers — you don't need to start from scratch.
