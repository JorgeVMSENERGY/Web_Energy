Who we are

Not just a supplier: a strategic partner. In a market that competes on volume and price, VMS Energy competes on the solution. Our starting point is not the equipment, it is the challenge of giving operations the certainty to grow safely, efficiently, and ethically.

VMS Energy is a Mexican company, founded in 2008, with 18 years of experience in engineering, technical supply, and construction (EPC) of electrical systems, electromechanical solutions, electrical infrastructure, Oil & Gas, solar generation, automation, and industrial lighting.

Our multidisciplinary team executes industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects with integrated, tailored solutions.

What we do

  • EPC: design, technical supply, and construction.
  • Power generation, transmission, and distribution.
  • Multi-brand automation and control, Oil & Gas, and industrial lighting.
  • Specialized supply: MCCs, switchgear, VFDs, regulators, actuators, and more.

Projects that define our capability

18 years backed by complex projects in the most demanding sectors. More Oil & Gas work is on our Oil & Gas page and in testing & commissioning.

Service · Offshore

Full rehabilitation of 14 transformers on the Balam-A platform

Challenge
Pemex offshore platform with 14 degraded transformers and no option to stop production for replacement.
Solution
In-situ rehabilitation: inspection, primary electrical testing (insulation resistance, TTR, power factor), commissioning, and return to service.
Result
Operational continuity maintained, with all transformers within Lloyd’s rules for shipboard electrical installations.

Campeche · Pemex / Cotemar

Project · Hydro

Cecilio del Valle hydroelectric plant modernization, CFE

Challenge
CFE hydro plant with obsolete speed control and hydraulic system, affecting frequency regulation on the grid.
Solution
Complete Woodward governor modernization and rehabilitation of the intake valve hydraulic control system.
Result
Stable digital speed control. The project was replicated at the Tamazulapan plant in Oaxaca.

Chiapas · CFE

Supply · Renewable

Wind farm: control and power systems supply

Challenge
Wind farm under construction requiring specialized control and power systems with critical delivery windows.
Solution
Integrated supply of electrical and control equipment: switchgear, protection, metering, and communication systems for the turbines.
Result
Delivered on schedule; the farm was commissioned with no supply-related delays.
