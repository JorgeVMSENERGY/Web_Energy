Our services

Turbine generator services

From parameter configuration to on-site commissioning, we provide complete technical support for governors, excitation, and the associated generation systems.

Governor programming and tuning

Configuration of speed and load PID parameters, droop, isochronous, and extraction control tuning on 505, 505-XT, 505E, and 2301D-ST governors. Woodward Toolkit used to download and upload configurations.

Commissioning and startup

Startup testing, overspeed trip verification, control loop tuning, and synchronization with the CFE grid. Includes load rejection testing and documentation of final parameters.

Synchronization and load control

Integration of EGCP-3 systems for automatic machine-to-grid and machine-to-machine paralleling. DSLC/MSLC configuration for isochronous load sharing without oscillation.

Mechanical governor retrofit

Upgrade of mechanical PGPL and PG-EG governors to 2301D or 505 electronic controllers, keeping the original hydraulic actuators. Eliminates speed drift and reduces maintenance.

Basler DECS voltage regulators (AVR)

Configuration of Basler DECS-250 and DECS-300 regulators for generator voltage control, power factor, and VAR sharing in parallel with CFE or in island mode.

Preventive maintenance and diagnostics

Inspection of control boards, LVDT electro-hydraulic actuators, MPU speed sensors, and backup batteries. Fault diagnosis from Woodward error codes and module replacement.

How we work

Service process

A structured methodology to minimize downtime during the harvest season and ensure reliable turbine generator operation.

1. Diagnosis and parameter reading

Download of the current configuration with Woodward Toolkit. Review of fault history, active PID parameters, and actuator condition. Analysis of current dynamic response vs. factory specifications.

2. Tuning and programming

Configuration of rated speed, acceleration limits, trip thresholds, speed and load PID loops, and droop or isochronous values according to the operating mode.

3. Field testing

Electronic overspeed test (110%), load rejection test, response time to load steps, and grid synchronization validation. Oscillogram recording.

4. Documentation and handover

Report with final parameters, test oscillograms, and configuration backup. Training for operations staff on basic governor monitoring and alarms.
