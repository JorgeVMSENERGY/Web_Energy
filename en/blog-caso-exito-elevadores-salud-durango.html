Seven elevators, four buildings and one manager: vertical transportation for the health services of the Durango

For the Coordinated Health Services of the State of Durango, VMS Energy carries out the diagnosis, repair, maintenance, replacement and installation of components and the commissioning of passenger elevators in three hospitals and in the central offices of the Secretariat, with personalized and permanent attention.

The challenge: elevators of different brands in hospitals that do not close

In a hospital, the elevator is part of the medical operation: patients on stretchers, supplies, and personnel are transported through it. An equipment out of service does not translate into a nuisance, but rather into transfers that are delayed or made through improvised routes.

The Coordinated Health Services of the State of Durango had passenger elevators in operation different manufacturers and times The hospital is divided into several buildings: the Cancerology Hospital, the Maternal and Child Hospital, the General Hospital 450 and the Central Offices of the Secretariat. Each brand has its own control, its own repairs and its own way of failing.

The assignment was to concentrate the specialized service of all this equipment in a single supplier: diagnose, repair, maintain, replace and install components and put into operation elevators, without relying on a different manufacturer for each.

The solution: a multi-brand service with permanent attention

VMS Energy assumed the specialized service of the seven elevators as a single care contract. Technical diagnosis of each equipment — movement, control, doors, safety and power supply — to distinguish what is resolved with maintenance from what requires repair or replacement components.

The diagnosis is carried out by the repair, the Maintenance and the replacement and installation of components and each intervention closes with the Commissioning elevator: adjustments, tests and return to service.

What the client highlights is the Personalized and permanent attention: a team that knows each property and each piece of equipment, with continuous monitoring and direct communication with the hospitals' maintenance areas.

What this means for your property

The Durango case shows a pattern that is repeated in hospitals, government and corporate buildings: over the years, the elevators in the same building end up being of different brands, ages and support levels. Managing a contract by manufacturer multiplies response times and responsible parties.

  • Multi-brand without conflict of interest: VMS Energy does not distribute or represent any elevator manufacturer, so the technical recommendation does not depend on selling a brand.
  • The elevator as an electrical system: as electrical contractors we also review the power supply, protections and backup of equipment, which in a hospital is as critical as the elevator itself.
  • One person responsible for all teams, it simplifies administration, monitoring and accountability.
