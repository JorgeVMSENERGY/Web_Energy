QHSE+ Comprehensive Management System

VMS Energy operates under a QHSE+ Integrated Management System that articulates five ISO standards in a single operating framework. Each EPC project — substations, automation, solar power, industrial HVAC or oil & gas — runs with the same certified standards of quality, safety, ethics, environment and energy efficiency.

Key differentiator in Mexico: few EPC integrators in the industrial electrical sector have all five certifications simultaneously. This translates to:

  • Documented compliance for corporate audits and due diligence.
  • Reduction of operational, environmental and compliance risks.
  • Measured, documented and auditable energy consumption in each EPC project.
  • Anti-bribery policies aligned with global corporate and public bidding requirements.

VMS Energy ISO Certifications FAQ

What is the QHSE+ Integral Management System of VMS Energy?

It is the management framework that integrates the five current ISO standards of VMS Energy into a single operating system: quality (9001), environment (14001), anti-bribery (37001), occupational health and safety (45001) and energy (50001). The acronym QHSE+ stands for Quality, Health, Safety & Environment, plus the anti-bribery and energy management dimensions. All EPC projects executed by VMS Energy operate under this system, regardless of the sector or size of the contract.

How many ISO certifications do VMS Energy have and what are these?

VMS Energy has five active ISO certifications issued by GSC (Global Anthrone-Ghne): ISO 9001:2015 (quality, registration GSC9KMX505), ISO 14001:2015(environment, GSC14KMX277), ISO 37001:2025 (antisoborn, GSC37KMX131), ISO 45001:2018 (safety and health, GSC45KMX182) and ISO 50001:2018 (energy, GSC50KMX101). All five were recertified on 23 October 2026 and are in force. until October 22, 2029. You can download each certificate in PDF from this page.

Do certifications apply to all EPC projects of VMS Energy?

Yes. The QHSE+ Integrated Management System is cross-sectional throughout the operation: engineering, technical supply, construction and maintenance. This means that a food plant automation project and an offshore platform electrical infrastructure project share the same certified quality, safety and ethical procedures.

Why is ISO 37001 (anti-susborn) important for tenders with CFE or Pemex?

ISO 37001 certifies that VMS Energy has documented bribery prevention policies throughout its chain: purchasing, subcontracting, relationship with public officials and commercial management. For public bidding processes under the Procurement Law or Works Law, this certification is evidence of anti-corruption compliance required with increasing frequency by buyers such as CFE Generación, Pemex and corporations with global due diligence standards.

What concrete advantage does ISO 50001 offer in an electrical engineering project?

ISO 50001 requires establishing a baseline of energy consumption, defining performance indicators (EnPIs) and demonstrating continuous improvement. In practical terms: any consumption reduction achieved in the project is documented and auditable by third parties. For companies with ESG commitments or GRI/CDP reporting, this translates into measurable evidence of energy efficiency that they can present to their own shareholders or customers.

How do I verify the validity of ISO certifications from VMS Energy?

Each certificate includes the official registration number issued by Global Standards Certification (GlobalSTD), certification body accredited by EMA, ANAB and IAF. You can download the PDFs directly from this page and verify their validity in the IAF global certification database (iafcertsearch.org) or with the certifying entity. To request additional copies or a letter of validity for your due diligence process, contact us at contacto@vmsenergy.com.
