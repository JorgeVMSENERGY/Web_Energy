    EHV Engineering Notes

    Key criteria for extra high voltage projects (66–400 kV)

    EHV projects require more than a cable provider. They require an integrator who coordinates detailed engineering, type testing, splicing, terminations and commissioning under a single person.

    • Type tests: Verify that the manufacturer has IEC 60840 / IEC 62067 tests in force for specific construction. Request laboratory reports, not just compliance statements.
    • Manufacturing times: Custom-made EHV cable requires between 16 and 28 weeks of factory manufacturing. This time must be built into the EPC schedule before committing to any go-live date.
    • Splices and terminations: They are a critical part of the system. VMS coordinates the supply of compatible accessories (pre-moulded splices, interior/outdoor finishes) and can run the installation with specialized personnel.
    • Applicable standards: IEC 60840 (66–150 kV), IEC 62067 (150–500 kV). For interconnections with CFE, review current LAPEM technical specifications. For projects with international financing, check if the bank requires IEEE or BS.
    • International logistics: Südkabel (Germany) and KEI (India) require coordination of import, customs clearance and specialized transport for large-format coils. VMS manages the entire process.

    Frequently Asked Questions — Extra High Voltage

    How long does it take to make an EHV cable?

    Between 16 and 28 weeks depending on voltage level, section and manufacturer. This time must be included in the EPC schedule from the basic engineering stage. VMS alerts about this period in the initial diagnosis.

    Which manufacturers drive for EHV?

    KEI Wires & Cables for most projects. Südkabel (Germany) for projects that require European manufacturing, VDE regulation or high engineering standards. The selection depends on the stress level, project standard and schedule.

    Does VMS also install the EHV cable or only supply it?

    VMS Energy is an EPC contractor. We can integrate supply, laying, splicing, terminations and high voltage tests (hipot, partial discharges) under a single contract. This integration eliminates the risk of interfaces between contractors.

    What minimum project justifies EHV management with VMS?

    There is no minimum size. We have coordinated everything from single circuit branches to complete transmission lines. What we do require is that the project has a technical specification or at least defined operating conditions to begin the diagnosis.
