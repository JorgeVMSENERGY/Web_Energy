Application context

ESP cables are not purchased in the catalog. They are specified for each well.

Each electric submersible pump (ESP) installation has unique conditions of temperature, pressure, well geometry and production profile. Incorrect cable selection is one of the most common causes of premature failure in ESP systems.

  • Fluid temperature: The thermal profile of the well determines the Artery family (180°F, 205°F, 290°F, 400°F or 450°F). Do not use surface temperature — use bottom hole temperature (BHT).
  • Well geometry: Deviated or short radius wells require Round cable for better mechanical performance. Vertical and straight wells allow Flat cable with greater space efficiency in the annulus.
  • Corrosive environment: Presence of H₂S, CO₂ or fluids with high salinity requires constructions with EPDM or Kapton insulation (families 400 and 450). Standard PP insulation (180/205 family) is not suitable for these environments.
  • Coordination with the ESP system: The cable must be compatible with the power of the motor, the drive (VSD) and the length of the well. VMS coordinates the specification with the ESP system manufacturer when the project requires it.

Integrated EPC

When VMS supplies the cable, you already know how it will be installed — because we are the ones who will install it.

Through VMShop you can request a cable quote directly — with or without an EPC project. If the supply is part of an EPC project that VMS engineers and builds, the cable is integrated into the MTO with milestone logistics and single responsibility. If you only need the material, we also support you with the same technical rigor.

  • Engineering: We specify the correct cable based on load, route, environment and regulations — not the cheapest cable in the catalog.
  • Coordinated MTO: The cable BOM is synchronized with the construction schedule, not the supplier's inventory.
  • Logistics by milestones: We deliver cable when the job needs it, in the correct quantity and with complete traceability per reel.
  • Sole responsibility: A single company responds by specification, supply, installation, testing and Aing.

If you only need uninstalled supplies, we can also support you — with the same technical and logistical rigors we apply to our EPC projects.

FAQ — ESP Cables

How do I select the right Arteria family for my well?

The main criterion is the Bottom Hole Temperature (BHT). Artery 180°F for normal conditions, 205°F for hotter wells, 290–450°F for high temperature wells and corrosive environments. VMS can help you select whether to share the well temperature profile.

Fat or Round for my installation?

Round is more flexible and recommended for deviated, short radius wells or wells with changes in direction. Flat takes up less space in the annulus of the well and can be more economical in vertical wells with sufficient space. The decision also depends on the diameter of the casing.

Do you manage availability in the field or only on request?

Some SKUs of 180 °F and 205 °F families are available in stock in Mexico. Higher temperature families (400°F and 450°F) are generally on-demand with lead times of 8 to 16 weeks. Check availability when quoting with specific SKU.

Does the cable include H2S protection?

The Arteria 400 and 450 families with EPDM insulation and Kapton (Polyimide) cover offer superior resistance to H₂S and corrosive fluids. For environments with high H₂S concentration, specify the KEFLG or KEFLEG variant. Consult VMS with well fluid analysis.
