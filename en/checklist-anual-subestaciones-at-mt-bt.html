Reference template for annual inspection and maintenance of industrial substations. Aligned with NOM-001-SEDE-2012, NOM-029-STPS-2011, IEEE C57, IEEE 80 and best industrial practices. This template is not a substitute for a formal protocol with calibrated test equipment — it is a roadmap for planning and auditing.
