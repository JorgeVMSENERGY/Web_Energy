What is VMShop?

Direct supply of industrial electrical equipment in catalogue

VMShop is the channel of Direct purchase from VMS Energy for line equipment: power cables, special ESP cables, photovoltaic cables and Triol frequency changers. No bidding process, no custom engineering — standard specification available for immediate delivery.

✓
VMShop — catalog equipment

Replacement of materials, maintenance stock, minor projects or complement of an EPC in execution where the material is specified. For: maintenance managers, technical buyers and project coordinators.

→
Formal technical procurement

Integrated supply to EPC projects with detail engineering, technical specification and custom critical equipment management (transformers, switchboards, MCC, instrumentation). See Process of Procura →

How does the process work?

01

Select

Explore the catalog by product line. Download the technical sheet and verify the specification for your application.

→
02

Request quote

Submit your materials list. A specialist confirms availability, minimum volume and delivery time in less than 24 hours.

→
03

Technical validation

Optional but recommended: Confirm the application with our engineering team before closing the order — at no additional cost.

→
04

Coordinated delivery

Shipping to the client's warehouse or directly to the project site in Mexico. Technical documentation and certificates included.
