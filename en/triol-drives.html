Frequently asked questions

Which Triol AT24 drive families do you offer?

UC for balanced industrial integrations, retrofits, and fast expansions; UH for heavy loads and severe duty cycles; and SD for large motors, high torque, and high energy demand.

What applications are they best for?

Conveyor belts and crushing, process pumping systems, and industrial and mine ventilation.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing is quoted per project. We keep in-stock inventory in Mexico for immediate delivery; models not in stock are quoted with lead time.

Do you support installation and startup?

Yes: technical support before the purchase order, integration with engineering specifications, and startup support, with in-stock inventory in Mexico for immediate delivery and spare parts.
