            4.1. Regarding VMS Energy customers

The main purpose of the data collected from clients is to establish formal commercial relationships, make quotes, integrate administrative files, and formalize contracts for the provision of services or sales. Specifically, the data is used to verify that the client is a legally constituted entity and registered with the Tax Administration Service, as well as to validate its tax domicile, its line of business or business activity, and its current tax situation through proof of tax situation and compliance opinion.

Institutional contact data, such as emails and telephone numbers of the sales, billing and accounts payable areas, are used to maintain efficient communication with the client, facilitate the tracking of commercial proposals and ensure the correct processing of payments. Likewise, bank details are requested to identify the institution issuing the payment, as well as to know from which account or bank the corresponding transfer will be made.

All this information is necessary for the proper integration of the customer into internal systems of management, production of purchase or sale orders, generation of electronic invoices and, where appropriate, to coordinate deliveries or provision of services. It is also used to ensure that the customer's data are up to date and complete, allowing for a transparent business relationship, efficient and in accordance with the law.

            4.2. In relation to VMS Energy suppliers

The data collected from VMS Energy suppliers is processed with the purpose of verifying their legal existence, commercial history and operational capacity, as well as to validate the authenticity of the information contained in their corporate, tax and banking documentation. Particularly, they are used to confirm the age of the company and prequalify it based on its experience; verify that the company name matches the one presented in quotes; validate the place where their offices are located and check that they correspond with what was declared in their proof of address; and review who will be the person authorized to sign contracts or purchase orders, including cases in which there are attorneys other than the legal representative.

Likewise, the data from VMS Energy suppliers is used to validate compliance with tax, social security and housing obligations, by obtaining and analyzing fiscal records such as the compliance opinion of the Tax Administration Service, as well as records from the Institute of the National Housing Fund for Workers and the Mexican Institute of Social Security. They are also used to verify the company's registration with the REPSE registry and ensure compliance with the applicable regulations on subcontracting, guaranteeing that the assigned personnel are not improperly subcontracted.

Regarding banking information, this is used to validate that payments are made to the corresponding account and that the beneficiary's data match the contracted company. Contact information allows you to establish and maintain ongoing communication with the supplier for administrative, contractual, logistical and compliance purposes. Finally, all the information collected is used for the purposes of preparing and formalizing contracts for the provision of services or sales, as well as to comply with the documentary requirements demanded by the parties for entry into facilities or for the execution of the contracted service.

Providers of technological, administrative or document management services, who due to the nature of their functions have access to information for secure storage, safeguarding, digital backup, database management, electronic billing platforms or other services linked to the company's internal management systems.

In all cases, data transfers will be carried out in accordance with the terms of this privacy notice, as established in the respective confidentiality contracts and in accordance with the provisions of the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. No additional data transfers will be made without the express consent of the owner, except in cases expressly authorized by said Law.
