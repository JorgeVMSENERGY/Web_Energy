The business case

When the engine fails, the plant stops

Motors represent 70% of industrial electricity consumption

In a typical plant, 70–80% of the electrical energy is consumed by the motors. Going from IE2 to IE3 reduces consumption by 3–6% per motor. With fleets of dozens of units, the return on investment is usually less than 18 months.

60% of motor failures are avoidable with the correct selection

Oversize, mis-chosen service factor, insufficient insulation class or standard engine in classified area are frequent errors. A poorly selected engine fails ahead of schedule and cancels the manufacturer's warranty.

A well-aligned engine lasts twice as long

Misalignment between motor and load causes 50% of premature bearing and mechanical seal failures. VMS Energy includes laser alignment and functional testing on every new installation — not as an extra, but as standard.

Scope of service

Supply, installation and service of engines

LV induction motors — 0.75 to 500 HP

Selection, supply and installation of low voltage three-phase induction motors (220–690 V) in NEMA or IEC housings. Applications in pumps, fans, compressors, conveyors and mixers. Brands: WEG W22, ABB M2BAX, Marathon Severe Duty.

IE3/IE4 high efficiency motors

Premium and Super Premium motors to reduce energy consumption and comply with international NOM and MEPS regulations. Comparative life cycle cost (LCC) assessment IE2 vs IE3 vs IE4 included. Typical savings: 3–8% of electric bill per motor replaced.

MV medium voltage motors — 2.3 to 13.8 kV

Supply and installation of MV engines for high-powered applications: gas compressors, transfer pumps, industrial mills and extrusoras. Powers from 200 HP to 10 000+ HP. Brands: WEG, Innomotics (Siemens), ABB. Including protection coordination with multi-function relief.

Motors for ATEX / NEC 500 classified area

Explosion-proof (Ex d), increased safety (Ex e) and non-scintillating (Ex n) motors for Oil & Gas facilities, chemical and grain processing plants. Temperature groups T3–T4. ATEX, IECEx and NEC 500 Class I Div 1 and Div 2 certifications. Brands: WEG Ex, Marathon DRIP-PROOF / TENV.

Installation, alignment and commissioning

Mechanical and electrical installation, precision laser alignment, phase current balance, insulation verification (megger), load functional test and thermal protection adjustment. Delivery with start-up protocol and registration of base parameters.

Rewinding and repair of industrial motors

Electrical and mechanical diagnosis, rewinding of LV/MV stators with high conductivity copper wire, replacement of bearings, VAC varnish treatment and hi-pot insulation tests. Repair with 12 month warranty. Own and third-party brands.

Methodology

How we manage your engine project

1. Selection engineering

Application survey: resistive torque curve, load cycle, installation environment, area classification, applicable standard (NEMA/IEC) and physical space restrictions. We provide calculation report and recommended engine sheet before quoting.

2. Supply and logistics

Management of purchase, import and delivery on site of the selected engine. Physical verification upon receipt: data plate, bearings, winding insulation and casing. For critical equipment we coordinate FAT at the manufacturer's plant if required.

3. Installation and alignment

Mechanical assembly, precision laser alignment (parallel and angular ≤ 0.05 mm), laying and connection of power and control wiring, adjustment of thermal protections and supervised start-up. Alignment protocol delivered as a quality document.

4. Start-up and monitoring

Full load functional test: measurement of phase currents, case temperature, base vibration (ISO 10816) and power factor. Recording of parameters as a baseline for future predictive maintenance. Post-delivery support included.
