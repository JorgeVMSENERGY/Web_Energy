Service scope

Electrical substation services

We cover the complete life cycle: from conceptual engineering to periodic major maintenance.

EPC construction

Conceptual and detailed design, supply of transformers, breakers, switchgear, and bus, civil works, electromechanical installation, FAT/SAT testing, and turnkey energization.

Rehabilitation and expansion

Replacement of breakers, disconnect switches, and transformers. Operating capacity expansion. Upgrade of protection and control systems (SEL and ABB REF numerical relays).

Major maintenance

Preventive and corrective maintenance: primary electrical testing, VLF, thermographic inspection, breaker cleaning and adjustment, protective relay calibration, and protection coordination.

Electrical acceptance testing

TTR (turns ratio), winding resistance, dielectric strength, no-load and load losses, VLF on MV and HV cables, short-circuit impedance test.

Synchronizing switchgear and interlocks

Design and installation of synchronizing switchgear for cogeneration systems and power plants. Safety interlocks and mechanical and electrical interlocking for shared substations.

ALS / SF₆ circuit breakers

Supply, installation, and rehabilitation of SF₆ high-voltage breakers (ALS 5315, ALS 56-235) for CFE Transmission and industrial substations. SF₆ refilling and testing.

EPC execution process

Engineering and design

Field survey, short-circuit study, protection coordination, single-line diagrams, calculation reports, and equipment specifications.

Equipment supply

Transformers, SF₆ or vacuum breakers, MV switchgear, bus, control and protection panels. FAT follow-up at the factory.

Electrical testing

Acceptance and diagnostic tests we perform

IEEE C57.12 / IEC 60076

TTR — Turns Ratio

Verifies the rated turns ratio and winding polarity on every tap of the DETC/OLTC.

NETA MTS / IEC 60076

Winding resistance

Detects loose joints, broken conductors, or thermal deterioration in windings. Measured with a Kelvin bridge or micro-ohmmeter.

IEEE 400.2 / IEC 60502

VLF — Very Low Frequency

0.1 Hz dielectric test on MV cables. Detects XLPE insulation deterioration without the destructive risk of DC testing.

IEC 60076-1 §11.5

Dielectric strength (hipot)

Voltage applied for 1 minute to verify the integrity of the main and phase-to-phase insulation.

ANSI/NETA MTS

Contact resistance

Measurement of main circuit resistance in breakers and disconnect switches to detect burnt or loose contacts.

IEC 62271 / ANSI C37

Opening/closing time

Verification of SF₆ and vacuum breaker operating times with a trip circuit analyzer.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about electrical substations

What does major substation maintenance include?
Major maintenance covers: primary electrical testing (TTR, winding resistance, dielectric strength), VLF testing of primary and secondary circuits, inspection and diagnosis of breakers and disconnect switches, adjustment and verification of protective relays, structure cleaning and painting, and a condition report with recommendations.
What is VLF testing in substations?
VLF (Very Low Frequency) is a dielectric test technique that applies AC voltage at a very low frequency (0.1 Hz) to MV cables and windings. It detects XLPE insulation deterioration without the destructive risk of traditional DC voltage. VMS Energy routinely applies it to primary and secondary circuits in industrial substations and offshore platforms.
Does VMS Energy build substations from scratch?
Yes. VMS Energy delivers complete EPC substation projects: conceptual and detailed engineering, equipment supply (transformers, SF₆ breakers, switchgear, bus), civil works, electromechanical installation, NETA acceptance testing, and commissioning. References include a substation built for PIASA in Veracruz and major maintenance of a 69 kV substation in Jalisco.
What voltage levels do you work with?
VMS Energy works with substations from medium voltage (4.16–34.5 kV) up to high voltage (115 kV). We have executed projects at 4,160 V, 6.6 kV, 13.8 kV, 23 kV, 34.5 kV, and 69 kV for industry, Oil & Gas, and sugar mills, as well as ALS breaker rehabilitation for CFE Transmission.
