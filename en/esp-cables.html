Application context

ESP cables are not bought from a catalog. They are specified for each well.

Every electrical submersible pump (ESP) installation has unique temperature, pressure, well geometry, and production profile conditions. Choosing the wrong cable is one of the most frequent causes of premature ESP system failure.

  • Fluid temperature: The well’s thermal profile determines the Arteria family (180 °F, 205 °F, 290 °F, 400 °F, or 450 °F). Do not use surface temperature — use bottom-hole temperature (BHT).
  • Well geometry: Deviated or short-radius wells require Round cable for better mechanical behavior. Straight vertical wells allow Flat cable, which uses annular space more efficiently.
  • Corrosive environment: H₂S, CO₂, or high-salinity fluids require constructions with EPDM or Kapton insulation (400 and 450 families). Standard PP insulation (180/205 families) is not suitable for these environments.
  • Coordination with the ESP system: The cable must be compatible with the motor power, the drive (VSD), and the well length. VMS coordinates the specification with the ESP system manufacturer when the project requires it.

Integrated EPC

When VMS supplies the cable, it already knows how it will be installed — because we are the ones installing it.

Through VMShop you can request a cable quote directly — with or without an EPC project. If the supply is part of an EPC project that VMS engineers and builds, the cable is integrated into the MTO with milestone-based logistics and single-point responsibility. If you only need the material, we support you with the same technical rigor.

  • Engineering: We specify the right cable for the load, route, environment, and code — not the cheapest cable in the catalog.
  • Coordinated MTO: The cable material take-off is synchronized with the construction schedule, not with the supplier’s inventory.
  • Milestone logistics: We deliver cable when the site needs it, in the right quantity, with full traceability per reel.
  • Single-point responsibility: One company answers for specification, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning.

If you only need supply without installation, we can help too — with the same technical and logistics rigor we apply to our EPC projects.

Frequently asked questions — ESP cables

How do I select the right Arteria family for my well?

The main criterion is bottom-hole temperature (BHT). Arteria 180 °F for normal conditions, 205 °F for hotter wells, and 290–450 °F for high-temperature wells and corrosive environments. VMS can help you select if you share the well’s temperature profile.

Flat or Round for my installation?

Round is more flexible and recommended for deviated, short-radius, or direction-changing wells. Flat takes less annular space and can be more economical in vertical wells with enough room. The decision also depends on the casing diameter.

Do you have field stock or only made to order?

Some SKUs in the 180 °F and 205 °F families are in stock in Mexico. Higher-temperature families (400 °F and 450 °F) are generally made to order, with lead times of 8 to 16 weeks. Check availability when you request a quote for a specific SKU.

Is the cable protected against H₂S?

Arteria 400 and 450 families with EPDM insulation and a Kapton (polyimide) jacket offer superior resistance to H₂S and corrosive fluids. For high H₂S concentrations, specify the KEFLG or KEFLEG variant. Consult VMS with the well fluid analysis.
