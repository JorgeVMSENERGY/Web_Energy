Engineering Notes

Key criteria for high voltage

The final selection depends on voltage level, installation method, protection coordination and voltage drop limits of the project.

  • Ampacity and temperature: validate permissible current of the driver at 90 °C in normal operation and 250 °C short circuit. Apply correction factors by ambient temperature, grouping and installation method (duct, tray, buried).
  • Construction and shielding: For underground installation in aggressive soils, specify steel reinforcement and HDPE cover. For installation on a tray with risk of humidity, validate longitudinal anti-humidity barrier.
  • Applicable standard: Projects with connection to CFE must comply with LAPEM specifications. Private projects: IEC 60502 (MV) or IEC 60840 (HV). Verify if the project requires NMX-J or international standard.
  • Logistics coordination: On-demand HV cable requires 8 to 14 weeks of manufacturing. Include this time in the EPC schedule before committing to a commissioning date.
  • QA/QC in reception: All VMS supplies include a certificate of test per coil, traceability labeling and check of the personal data of the company before installation.

Frequently asked questions

What information do I need to quote HV cable?

Voltage level (U0/U in kV), total length per section, type of installation (buried, tray, duct), required standard (IEC, NMX-J, CFE/LAPEM), ambient site temperature and required delivery schedule.

Can you coordinate deliveries by stage of construction?

Yes. We schedule milestone deliveries with coil traceability and complete documentation (test certificates, MTC, packaging). This is standard in all our EPC projects.

What's the difference between armored and armorless wire?

The armor (galvanized steel wires) mechanically protects against impacts and crushing. It is mandatory for directly buried installation and recommended in ducts with risk of mechanical damage. In an inner tray without mechanical risk, it is generally not necessary.

Can I use aluminum conductors instead of copper?

Yes. Aluminium reduces driver cost but requires larger sections for equal ampacity (approximately 1.5 times). The decision depends on the balance between cost, weight, available space in trays and customer requirements or project standard.

What documentation does VMS deliver with the supply?

Factory test certificate (type and individual per coil), cable data sheet, report of measured lengths, traceability labeling per batch and, if the project requires it, documentation for CFE/PEMEX.

Do they also install the cable or only supply it?

Both options are possible. VMS Energy is an EPC contractor — if the project requires it, we integrate supply, laying, splicing, terminations and testing under a single contract. If you only need supply, we also do it with the same technical rigor.
