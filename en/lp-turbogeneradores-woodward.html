Our services

Turbogeneration services

From parameter configuration to plant commissioning, we offer complete technical support on governors, excitation and associated generation systems.

Governor programming and adjustment

Configuration of speed and load PID parameters, droop adjustment, isochrone and extraction control on 505, 505-XT, 505E and 2301D-ST governors. Using Woodward Toolkit to download and upload configurations.

Commissioning and start-up

Startup tests, overspeed trip verification, control loop adjustment and synchronization with CFE network. Includes load rejection testing and final parameter documentation.

Synchronization and load control

Integration of EGCP-3 systems for automatic control of machine-network and machine-machine parallelism. DSCL/MSCL configuration for isochronous load sharing without oscillations.

Retrofit of mechanical governors

Modernization of mechanical PGPL and PG-EG governors to 2301D or 505 electronic controllers, preserving original hydraulic actuators. Eliminates speed drift and reduces maintenance.

Basler DECS Voltage Regulators (AVR)

Configuration of Basler DECS-250 and DECS-300 regulators for generator voltage control, power factor and VAR sharing in parallel operation with CFE or islanded.

Preventive maintenance and diagnosis

Inspection of control cards, LVDT electrohydraulic actuators, MPU speed sensors and backup batteries. Fault diagnosis by Woodward error codes and module replacement.

How we work

Service process

Structured methodology to minimize downtime during the harvest and guarantee reliable operation of the turbogenerator.

1. Diagnosis and reading of parameters

Download current configuration with Woodward Toolkit. Reading failure history, active PID parameters and actuator status. Current vs. dynamic response analysis factory specifications.

2. Adjustment and programming

Configuration of nominal speed parameters, acceleration limits, trip thresholds, speed and load PID loops, and droop or isochrone values ​​depending on the operating mode.

3. Field tests

Electronic overspeed test (110%), load rejection test, response time verification to load steps and network synchronization validation. Oscillogram recording.

4. Documentation and transfer

Report delivery with final parameters, test oscillograms and configuration backup. Training of operating personnel in basic monitoring and governor alarms.
