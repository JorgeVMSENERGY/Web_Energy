Competitive advantage

Civil works and electrical installation in a single proposal

Separating the civil contractor from the electrical engineer generates interference, downtime and diffuse responsibilities. VMS Energy covers both disciplines under a single firm, reducing coordination risks and the total project time.

One technical-economic proposal

Civil + electromechanical + HVAC in a single document. Less administrative burden for the client and greater control of the total scope.

Coordination of disciplines from day one

Ducts, pipelines and foundations are designed considering the final electrical load. No re-work due to incompatibility between specialties.

Sole responsibility to the client

VMS Energy is the total delivery manager: time, quality and budget. No friction between subcontractors to justify deviations.

Scope of services

Infrastructure and industrial conditioning services

Civil works for electrical installations

Foundations, pedestals and bases for substations, transformers and generating sets. Underground conduits for MV/HV power cables. Electrical rooms and control booths with ventilation, emergency lighting and access control.

Electromechanical design of facilities

Integrated design of electrical, mechanical and piping systems for industrial plants. Preparation of construction plans, calculation reports and technical specifications in compliance with NOM-001-SEDE, NFPA 70 and IEC.

Industrial and Hospital HVAC Systems

Design, specification, installation and commissioning of industrial air conditioning systems. Electrical rooms, clean rooms, ATEX areas, hospitals and data centers. Thermal load calculation, duct design and equipment selection.

Space conditioning

Adaptation of rooms for panels, UPS, data centers and HVAC systems. Waterproofing, anti-dust finishes, conductive floors, and support structures for cable trays and process equipment.

EPC turnkey projects

Civil integration for complete EPC projects: from basic and detailed engineering to delivery with acceptance tests (FAT/SAT). Permit management, coordination with CFE and delivery of as-built file.

Construction of electrical rooms and control rooms

Construction of modular electrical rooms with metal structure, sandwich panel, precision air conditioning and fire detection-suppression system. Installation of gutters, trays and technical false floor.
